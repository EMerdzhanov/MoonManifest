About

Moon Manifest helps you align your intentions with the moon's natural rhythm. Set your intentions during the New Moon, take action during the Waxing phase, celebrate during the Full Moon, and release what no longer serves you during the Waning phase.

Features

  • Real-time moon phase tracking with beautiful visualizations
  • Guided manifestation prompts tailored to each lunar phase
  • Voice-to-text input for speaking your manifestations
  • Daily lunar wisdom and guidance
  • Manifestation history and journal
  • Home screen widget showing the current moon phase
  • Available in 11 languages
