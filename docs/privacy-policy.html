Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Moon Manifest ("the App") is developed by X8 Dev. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

Moon Manifest does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The App operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any servers.

Data Storage

All data you create within the App — including intentions, gratitude entries, cycle history, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

Speech Recognition

The App offers an optional voice input feature for entering gratitude text. Speech recognition is processed entirely on your device using the operating system's built-in speech services. No audio data is recorded, stored, or transmitted by the App.

Data Export

The App allows you to export your data as an encrypted file. This export is initiated solely by you and saved to a location you choose on your device. The exported file is encrypted with AES-256 using a password you provide.

Notifications

The App may send local notifications to remind you of your manifestation practice. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device and do not involve any external services.

Third-Party Services

Moon Manifest does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud services.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
